Privacy Policy

Zero MedRX · Effective 2026-10-09
Your health data stays on your phone. Your medicines, schedules, dose history and health readings are stored only on your device. We have no account system and no server copy of them. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and a few features use the internet as described below.

1. What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone, in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. The app never copies it to the cloud on its own; copies exist only if you make a backup (see “Backups you make” below):

  • Medicines you add (name, strength, type, notes, optional photo link, schedule)
  • Dose history (Taken / Snoozed / Skipped / Missed) and streaks
  • Profiles you create (a name and an emoji)
  • Health readings, pill stock, routines and settings you enter
  • Adherence reports (PDF) you create — shared only if you tap Share
  • Caregiver contacts you enter (email or phone and an optional name)
  • The downloaded medicine list for your country

Medicine photos are chosen with Android’s photo picker; the app keeps only a link to the photo and never uploads it.

Backups you make

In Settings → Backup & restore you can save a copy of the data above as one backup file, and restore it later on any phone. A backup goes only where you choose to save it — for example your phone, Google Drive or another app — and is never sent to us. With Premium, you can also turn on a daily automatic backup into a folder you pick. You can protect backups with a password: the file is then encrypted (AES-256), and your password is never stored or sent anywhere, so a forgotten password can’t be recovered. Backups do not include your Premium purchase, your ad choices or cached data. Anyone who gets a backup file without a password can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

You can turn the medicine list off in Settings → Medicine list country.

3. Third-party services

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and Google Play services — policies.google.com/privacy (how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • openFDA — open.fda.gov/terms

4. Advertising

The free version shows a banner ad on the main screens. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and older. Google may use your device’s advertising ID to show personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads). Buying Premium removes all ads.

6. Children’s privacy

Zero MedRX is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. You may add family members, including children, as profiles; their information stays on your device like everything else in section 1.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes it immediately.
  • Backup files: stay wherever you saved them until you delete them there; uninstalling the app does not remove them. Automatic backups keep the newest 7 files in the folder you picked.
  • Push token and caregiver alerts: stored in our Firebase project only to deliver notifications. To have them deleted, email us (section 11).
  • Ads, Google Play and openFDA: kept according to those providers’ policies (section 3).

8. Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

You can ask us to access or delete the little data we hold on our side (your push token and any caregiver alerts) by emailing us. Everything else you can view, edit or delete yourself in the app. You can control ad personalisation in your Android settings (section 4). We do not sell or share personal information for money.

9. Security

Data on your phone is kept in Android’s app-private storage, which other apps cannot read without root access. All network connections use encryption (HTTPS/TLS).
