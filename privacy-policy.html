Privacy Policy

Medicine Reminder · Effective 2026-04-16
Medicine Reminder collects nothing. Stores everything on your device only. Sends nothing anywhere. We don’t have your medicines, dosages, schedules, or intake history — only your phone does. Uninstall the app and every trace of your data is gone.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Medicine Reminder does not collect any personal data, usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or any other information from you or your device.

2. What is stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your phone only, in app-private storage that no other app can read:

  • Medicine entries you create (name, dosage, optional notes, schedule times)
  • Intake records (Taken / Snoozed / Skipped / Missed) and their timestamps
  • Your preferences (snooze duration, grace period)
  • App state (e.g. whether you’ve completed onboarding)

This data never leaves your device. We have no servers and no way to access it.

3. Third-party services

None. Medicine Reminder does not include analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, cloud-storage or backend services, social-login providers, or any third-party SDK that sends data off the device.

4. Network access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission. It is technically incapable of sending any data off your device.

5. Permissions used and why

PermissionWhy the app asks for it
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) To display reminder notifications when a dose is due.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM (Android 12+) To fire reminders at the exact minute you scheduled them.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To re-arm pending reminders after you restart your phone.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS (optional) To prevent Android from silencing reminders during Doze.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, calendar, network, or any other permission beyond those above.

6. Children’s privacy

Medicine Reminder is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because we collect no information at all.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives only on your device:

  • Retention is entirely under your control. Data persists until you delete it (medicine-by-medicine in the app, or all at once by clearing the app’s data in Android Settings).
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app removes all data immediately. There is no off-device copy of anything to request deletion of, because there is no off-device copy.

8. Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws)

Because Medicine Reminder collects and processes no personal data on our side, GDPR/CCPA data-subject rights do not result in any action we need to take on your behalf:

  • Right to access: there is no data on our side to disclose.
  • Right to rectify: edit your entries directly in the app.
  • Right to delete: uninstall the app, or clear app data in Android Settings.
  • Right to portability: not applicable; we hold no data.
  • Right to object / restrict processing: not applicable; we do no processing.

If a future version introduces optional cloud features, those will be opt-in only, and this policy will be updated to describe what data those features handle, before any data is collected.

9. Security

Even though no data leaves your device, we still take care of what’s on it. The app uses Android’s app-private storage; data is not readable by other apps without root access. Future cloud features (when introduced as opt-in) will use TLS in transit and at-rest encryption.

10. Changes to this policy

If anything material changes — for example, if a future version introduces network features — we will post an updated policy at the same URL with a new effective date, and surface a notice inside the app before any new data collection begins.
